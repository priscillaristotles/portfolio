Case Study 04

B2B Go-to-Market from Zero for an Early-Stage SaaS Platform

Commercial & B2B Sales
Early-Stage SaaS · SEA
Details anonymized

The Problem

A new B2B subscription product needed to acquire 300 paying studio customers in 12 months - with one solo operator, a phased budget starting under $200/month, and a product that wasn't yet fully optimal. No SDR team, no marketing budget, no GTM playbook.

What I Did

  • Designed an outcome-gated GTM playbook with milestone-based budget unlocks
  • Built a 6-channel mix sequenced by phase, not launched simultaneously
  • Engineered a 4-touch outreach sequence with localized templates
  • Created a 20-minute discovery framework that diagnoses bottleneck type
  • Built a custom lightweight CRM pre-loaded with qualified leads
  • Drafted a phased budget proposal for CEO with hard kill switches

Results

  • Structured a 4-gate phase ladder from 0 → 300 paid customers, each unlocked by hard milestone
  • Reduced approval friction by proposing budget in tranches starting at <$200/mo, scaling only on proof
  • Mapped 6 acquisition channels with attribution targets, CAC ceilings, and kill switches
  • Delivered 17-section operating playbook + functional CRM as live deliverables
  • Strategy currently in execution; CEO-approved framework, milestones tracked weekly

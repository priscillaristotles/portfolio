About

I'm a systems thinker who happens to love building things from the ground up.

I started my career as a business intelligence analyst in Austria, learning to find the signal inside messy operational data. That instinct never left me — but I wanted to build, not just measure.

So I founded Samasta Aerial Studio out of a personal passion for aerial arts, and grew it into seven branches across four Indonesian cities in about a year and a half. Running a multi-location business taught me everything analysis couldn't: how to design systems people will actually follow, how to hire, how to keep quality consistent at scale.

Then something unusual happened. I became the best customer of a startup called Kuyy — and they asked me to come run their business side. Today I'm their Chief Business Officer, leading go-to-market and an active fundraise. I've sat on both sides of the table: the operator buying the software, and the executive building it.

That's the lens I bring to every project — someone who's done the unglamorous work of making a business run, not just advised on it.
